Introduction:
JUDGIFY is an AI-powered legal assistant developed using Natural Language Processing (NLP) to help users understand legal issues in a simple and accessible way. The system analyzes user queries related to crimes, cyber fraud, theft, banking issues, and other legal concerns, then provides relevant laws, explanations, confidence scores, and suggested actions. Built using Flask, Python, and NLP techniques such as tokenization, preprocessing, and intent matching, the chatbot offers instant legal guidance through an interactive web-based interface. The project aims to simplify legal awareness and make basic legal assistance available to everyone without requiring expert legal knowledge.

Problem Statement:
Many people lack awareness of legal procedures and often struggle to identify the correct law or action for issues such as theft, cyber fraud, account blocking, and robbery. Accessing legal advice can be time-consuming, expensive, and confusing due to complex legal terminology. Existing systems are not always user-friendly or easily accessible for common users. Therefore, there is a need for an intelligent NLP-based legal assistant that can understand user queries in natural language and provide quick, accurate, and simplified legal guidance along with relevant laws and suggested next steps.
